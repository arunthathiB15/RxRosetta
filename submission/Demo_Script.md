# RxRosetta demo video script (target 2:30, screen recording with voiceover)

**0:00 The problem (15s).** Show the Papers tab. "Meet Kamala, 72, discharged with seven new medicines on a paper she can barely read. Half of long-term patients don't take medicines as prescribed. RxRosetta fixes the gap between hospital and home."

**0:15 Papers (25s).** Click the "Heart attack discharge" sample. "Indian formats like 1-0-1, brand names like Ecosprin and Pan, even spelling mistakes are understood." Click Make my plan.

**0:40 My plan (30s).** Walk the four time columns. Point at food rules and purpose chips. Switch to Tamil, then Hindi. Click Read aloud. "Kamala sees large text and hears it in her language."

**1:10 Safety checks (30s).** Scroll to Safety checks. "Ibuprofen with aspirin and clopidogrel raises bleeding risk. Two acid reducers are listed together. Every warning links to the official DailyMed label, and nothing comes from a model's memory."

**1:40 Teach-back and caregiver (25s).** Open Teach-back, answer one wrong on purpose. Open the Caregiver tab in the second window. "A low score alerts her daughter, live."

**2:05 Check-in (15s).** Tick "Unusual bleeding or black stools". Show the emergency banner, then the alert on the caregiver tab. "Triage is rule-based and uses her own medicines."

**2:20 Evidence and close (10s).** Open Evaluation. "94.3% recall on 40 synthetic noisy notes, with failures shown openly. Decision support, not medical advice. RxRosetta: clear, safe medicines at home."

**Recording tips:** use the Heart attack sample for the whole demo, keep the browser at 125% zoom, record in one take per section, and upload to YouTube as unlisted for Devpost.
